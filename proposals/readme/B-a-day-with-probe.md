---

### <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="../../assets/probe-lost-dark.svg"><img src="../../assets/probe-lost.svg" width="28" align="absmiddle" alt=""></picture> &nbsp;07:00 — the flood

**80 new papers** on `cs.RO` + `cs.LG` overnight. Maybe three touch dexterous
manipulation. You skim twelve titles and remember none.

### <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="../../assets/probe-scouting-dark.svg"><img src="../../assets/probe-scouting.svg" width="28" align="absmiddle" alt=""></picture> &nbsp;07:05 — the sweep

The scheduled routine reads **one pillar** of `context/`, sweeps the day and
lands a scored shortlist in `scouting/P#/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — each paper tied to
the open decision it bears on.

<picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="../../assets/tagline-dark.svg"><img src="../../assets/tagline.svg" width="880" alt="The day narrows to a shortlist. Scored, tied to your open decisions, already in your repo."></picture>

### <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="../../assets/probe-analysis-dark.svg"><img src="../../assets/probe-analysis.svg" width="28" align="absmiddle" alt=""></picture> &nbsp;09:30 — the read

One of the three is worth it. `/analyze <arXiv id>` rewrites it in Korean from
the arXiv HTML original — a one-screen 요약 on top, the full body below.
Coffee optional.

### <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="../../assets/probe-comparison-dark.svg"><img src="../../assets/probe-comparison.svg" width="28" align="absmiddle" alt=""></picture> &nbsp;14:00 — the weighing

Two papers claim the same thing. `/compare <id> <id>` puts them under **one
question** and writes only where they part ways.

### <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="../../assets/probe-presentation-dark.svg"><img src="../../assets/probe-presentation.svg" width="28" align="absmiddle" alt=""></picture> &nbsp;Friday — the talk

Lab meeting. `/present <id>` recuts the rewrite as slides — 起承轉結, a
speaker essay under every one.

### <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="../../assets/human-dark.svg"><img src="../../assets/human.svg" width="28" align="absmiddle" alt=""></picture> &nbsp;Always — the part that stays yours

`context/` — your pillars, your Decision Log. The agent reads it
every run and **never** edits it.
