## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: the questions of what happens to sensing **before** it reaches the
policy. **How views are tied** — whether multiple cameras reach the policy as
one spatially registered embedding or as separate images. **How modalities
combine** — vision, proprioception, tactile and force, and whether contact
stays attributed to the finger it belongs to. **What the encoder already
knows** — dynamics, geometry, or generic image features. The encoder
*choice* is P2's; the VLM *weights and lineage* are P4's.

**Thesis tie** — why this axis is asked: a flat concat discards the spatial
relation between views and which finger a contact belongs to, and whether
dexterity depends on what it discards is open — so how the observation is
built is asked deliberately, and what it builds is what the action decoder
reads (P1 D9ZP). Flat concat and a generic image stem stay threads, the
baselines a structured observation is measured against.

**Questions**: how camera views are tied (D8EJ), what the vision encoder
already knows (D9NB), how modalities are combined (D2DV), how hand sensing
becomes tokens (D3AG).

**Premises** — the hand is a **high-DOF multi-fingered hand** whose
fingertips carry dense, vision-based tactile sensing at camera rate.

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **Structure that survives to the policy input** — registration kept across
   views, contact attributed to a named finger or site, an encoder that
   already carries dynamics or geometry. The north star.
2. **Any fusion beyond flat concat** — gating, cross-attention, asymmetric
   routing, modality dropout, learned tactile representations — counted even
   when the seam is not per-finger, because what a structure buys transfers
   across seams.
3. **Flat concat / token-append and vision-only baselines** — the comparison
   group. A paper that *measures* what appending costs is worth more here
   than one that merely asserts it.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P2
or a P2 question):

- Vision-only manipulation with no multimodal or spatial-fusion contribution
- Flat-concat fusion presented as sufficient without measuring what it loses
  in spatial registration or per-finger contact attribution
- Generic image encoders pitched as sufficient for dexterity without a
  measurement against an action- or geometry-aware stem
- Pure tactile-only without structured binding; 2-finger parallel-jaw grippers
  only
- Tactile sensing hardware design without a learned representation / encoder
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Open Questions — P2 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The questions this pillar keeps open. Each names what it turns on and the
threads the corpus has followed so far; none is waiting to be closed, and a
thread is added when a paper opens one.

#### [D8EJ] 여러 카메라 시점을 정책 앞에서 어떻게 하나의 공간으로 묶나 (P2)
- Per-camera tokens concatenated leave the policy to recover how views relate. Threads: a geometry-grounded multi-view encoder giving one 3D-consistent embedding (VGGT, eVGGT); views tied by their known projective relation (G3VLA); a cross-attention fuser; views tied by training with no calibration (AnyViewDex).
#### [D9NB] 시각 인코더는 무엇을 미리 알고 있어야 하나 — 동역학, 기하, 일반 이미지 (P2)
- A generic image stem knows what things look like, not how they move under action. Threads: dynamics put inside the encoder (DynaFLIP); geometry distilled into it (eVGGT); next-frame structure learned from video alone (TDV); a generic SigLIP / ImageNet stem.
#### [D2DV] 서로 다른 감각을 어떻게 합치나 — 이어 붙이기 너머 (P2)
- Appending a modality as more tokens has a measured cost, and binding it costs design. Threads: cross-attention / asymmetric fusion with modal masking (ForceFlow, ViTacFormer); binding in place of appending at the same sequence length (AT-VLA); contact-gated weighting of vision and touch (CAAT); modality dropout; flat concat.
#### [D3AG] 손의 감각을 어떤 토큰으로 만드나 — 손가락 단위 결속과 손의 구조 (P2)
- Which finger a contact belongs to, and how the fingers relate, is lost unless the tokens keep it. Threads: per-finger proprio-tactile tokens behind a swappable sensor head (TactX); finger identity and kinematic chain encoded with self-attention across fingers; hand-level aggregation from kinematics (XL-VLA); contact and slip auxiliary heads.
