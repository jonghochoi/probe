## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: what happens to sensing **before** it reaches the policy, on three
axes. **(a) Multi-camera spatial-geometric grounding** — multiple views fused
into one 3D-consistent embedding (camera / point / depth-aligned features),
cross-view registration kept rather than per-camera tokens concatenated.
**(b) Heterogeneous modality fusion beyond concat** — cross-attention /
asymmetric fusion of vision + proprioception + tactile + force that keeps
per-finger and palm contact attribution (each finger's joint state bound to
that finger's tactile feature; ~10 finger + 2 palm tokens; topology-aware).
**(c) Action/dynamics-aware vision encoder** — a stem pretrained on how a
scene changes under action, or on geometry, over a generic image encoder. The
encoder *choice* is P2's; the VLM *weights and lineage* are P4's.

**Thesis tie**: dexterity depends on exactly what a flat concat discards — the
spatial relation between views, and which finger a contact belongs to. So the
observation is elevated deliberately rather than left for the policy to
recover, and the fused spatial-contact embedding is what the Body/Hand decoder
(P1 D9ZP) reads.

**Tracked items**: multi-camera spatial-geometric grounding (D8EJ),
action/dynamics-aware vision encoder (D9NB), heterogeneous modality fusion
beyond concat (D2DV), proprio-tactile-force token construction (D3AG),
topology-aware encoding + hand-level aggregation (D9WZ).

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **Structure that survives to the policy input** — registration kept across
   views, contact attributed to a named finger or site, an encoder that
   already carries dynamics or geometry. The north star.
2. **Any fusion beyond flat concat** — gating, cross-attention, asymmetric
   routing, modality dropout, learned tactile representations — counted even
   when the seam is not per-finger, because what a structure buys transfers
   across seams.
3. **Flat concat / token-append and vision-only baselines** — the comparison
   group. A paper that *measures* what appending costs is worth more here
   than one that merely asserts it.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P2
or a P2 Decision):

- Vision-only manipulation with no multimodal or spatial-fusion contribution
- Flat-concat fusion that loses spatial registration or per-finger contact
  attribution
- Generic non-action-aware image encoders pitched as sufficient for dexterity
- Pure tactile-only without structured binding; 2-finger parallel-jaw grippers
  only
- Tactile sensing hardware design without a learned representation / encoder
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Decision Log — P2 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The current choice per decision, **bold** on the chosen alternative, deferred
alternatives inline; rationale lives outside this file.
P2 owns **D8EJ–D9WZ** — the full allocation is in `context/CLAUDE.md`.

> **The hand these decisions are written against**: near-term the **Sharpa
> Hand** — 22 DOF, no wrist DOF, fingertip Deform Map tactile (vision-based,
> ~320×240 per fingertip @30 Hz) — with **xhand** as the alternate and an
> a custom hand later, spec open. D3AG's swappable sensor head + common token
> format is what absorbs a change of hand, and its first non-negotiable — no
> Sharpa lock-in — is why.

#### [D8EJ] Multi-camera spatial-geometric grounding (P2)
- geometry-grounded multi-view encoder → **unified 3D-consistent spatial embedding** (VGGT/eVGGT-style camera/point/depth-aligned features) before the VLM, replacing flat per-camera concat. Keep cross-view registration; (ii) cross-attention fuser retained as the lightweight fallback
#### [D9NB] Action/dynamics-aware vision encoder (P2)
- prefer an **action/dynamics-aware encoder** (DynaFLIP tri-modal-dynamics family) or geometry-distilled encoder (eVGGT) over a generic SigLIP/ImageNet stem. **Boundary**: the *observation encoder* choice is P2; the *VLM backbone weights/lineage* (D3RP) are P4
#### [D2DV] Heterogeneous modality fusion beyond concat (P2)
- **cross-attention / asymmetric fusion** of vision + proprio + tactile + force (ForceFlow-style modal masking / AdaLN), not flat concat; modality dropout for graceful degradation
#### [D3AG] Proprio-tactile-force token construction (P2)
- per-finger proprio-tactile binding, 10 finger + 2 palm tokens (both hands); hardware-specific CNN on Deform Map → per-fingertip feature → finger token; **swappable sensor head + common token format**; contact-binary + slip-binary aux heads (light). Non-negotiable: (1) no Sharpa lock-in, (2) preserve contact-relevant features
#### [D9WZ] Topology-aware encoding + hand-level aggregation (P2)
- (iii) full topology-aware encoding (finger/hand identity, palm-relative fingertip pose, kinematic chain) + (B) self-attention over finger/palm tokens for hand-level aggregation
