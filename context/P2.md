## 1. Identity [STABLE] [AGENT-INPUT]

> Most VLA-style policies feed sensing as a **flat concat / token-append** of vision-dominant streams over a generic, non-action-aware image encoder — and so discard exactly what dexterity depends on: the *spatial* relation between camera views and the *contact* semantics carried by proprioception, tactile, and force. Dexterous **hand** manipulation must instead **elevate the observation itself**, on three axes: (a) **multi-camera spatial-geometric grounding** — register the 3D structure across views into one embedding rather than concatenating per-camera tokens; (b) **heterogeneous modality fusion beyond concat** — fuse vision + proprioception + tactile + force through structured cross-attention while preserving per-finger/palm contact attribution; (c) an **action/dynamics-aware vision encoder** — a representation that already encodes how a scene changes under action, not merely what objects are present. Task specification stays goal-centric (arm-hand integrated).

**Decomposition (P2-relevant)**
- *Antagonist*: vision-only manipulation; flat-concat multimodal fusion that loses spatial registration and per-finger contact attribution; generic non-action-aware image encoders treated as sufficient
- *Protagonist (P2 owns)*: geometry-grounded multi-camera spatial fusion + cross-attention heterogeneous (vision/proprio/tactile/force) fusion + an action/dynamics-aware encoder, with per-finger/palm structured tactile tokens as the contact branch
- *Downstream context*: the fused spatial-contact embedding feeds the heterogeneous Body/Hand decoder (P1 D4) under a preserved VLM prior (full doc P4); the encoder *choice* is P2, the VLM *weights/lineage* are P4

> **Note**: P2 owns the observation-fusion half. The decoder split (P1),
> System0 (P3), pretraining/preservation (P4), datasets (P0), world model (P5)
> are out of scope here — see `context/MASTER.md`.

## 2. Pillar P2 — Structured Multimodal Observation Fusion [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Scope**: elevate the observation along three axes. **(a) Multi-camera spatial-geometric grounding** — fuse multiple camera views into a 3D-consistent unified spatial embedding (geometry-grounded encoders à la VGGT: camera/point/depth-aligned features), preserving cross-view registration rather than flat per-camera concat. **(b) Heterogeneous modality fusion beyond concat** — cross-attention / asymmetric fusion of vision + proprioception + tactile + force, keeping per-finger/palm contact attribution (each finger's joint state + that finger's tactile feature → one local embedding; ~10 finger + 2 palm tokens; topology-aware). **(c) Action/dynamics-aware vision encoder** — prefer an encoder pretrained to encode motion/dynamics (DynaFLIP-style) or geometry (eVGGT) over a generic image encoder. The encoder choice lives here; the VLM backbone weights/lineage live in P4.

**Identity tie**: hand-level "observation elevation" — spatial information *kept and registered*, contact semantics *attributed per finger*, and the encoder *action-aware* — not implicitly learned from flat concat.

**Tracked items**: multi-camera spatial-geometric grounding (D8), action/dynamics-aware vision encoder (D9), heterogeneous modality fusion beyond concat (D10), proprio-tactile-force token construction (D11), topology-aware encoding + hand-level aggregation (D12).

**Anti-topics**: vision-only manipulation; flat-concat fusion that loses spatial registration or per-finger attribution; generic non-action-aware encoders pitched as sufficient; tactile-hardware design without a learned representation.

**Literature anchor**: VGGT (multi-view geometry grounding), eVGGT / Geometry-Aware Vision Encoder (geometry encoder for manipulation), DynaFLIP (action/dynamics-aware tri-modal encoder), ForceFlow (asymmetric multimodal fusion), ViTacFormer (cross-attention visuotactile). See §5.

#### [D8] Multi-camera spatial-geometric grounding (P2)
- **v1**: geometry-grounded multi-view encoder → **unified 3D-consistent spatial embedding** (VGGT/eVGGT-style camera/point/depth-aligned features) before the VLM, replacing flat per-camera concat. Keep cross-view registration; (ii) cross-attention fuser retained as the lightweight fallback
#### [D9] Action/dynamics-aware vision encoder (P2)
- **v1**: prefer an **action/dynamics-aware encoder** (DynaFLIP tri-modal-dynamics family) or geometry-distilled encoder (eVGGT) over a generic SigLIP/ImageNet stem. **Boundary**: the *observation encoder* choice is P2; the *VLM backbone weights/lineage* (D19) are P4
#### [D10] Heterogeneous modality fusion beyond concat (P2)
- **v1**: **cross-attention / asymmetric fusion** of vision + proprio + tactile + force (ForceFlow-style modal masking / AdaLN), not flat concat; modality dropout for graceful degradation
#### [D11] Proprio-tactile-force token construction (P2)
- **v1**: per-finger proprio-tactile binding, 10 finger + 2 palm tokens (both hands); hardware-specific CNN on Deform Map → per-fingertip feature → finger token; **swappable sensor head + common token format**; contact-binary + slip-binary aux heads (light). Non-negotiable: (1) no Sharpa lock-in, (2) preserve contact-relevant features
#### [D12] Topology-aware encoding + hand-level aggregation (P2)
- **v1**: (iii) full topology-aware encoding (finger/hand identity, palm-relative fingertip pose, kinematic chain) + (B) self-attention over finger/palm tokens for hand-level aggregation
