## 1. Identity [STABLE] [AGENT-INPUT]

> Genesis-style labs spend almost everything **upstream, on pretraining**, and recover it as data-efficient adaptation — minutes of deploy data, not weeks. The earlier P4 chased the *symptom* (catastrophic forgetting under deploy fine-tuning) and the reflex "just scale the deploy data"; the real lever sits one stage earlier. A hand-centric VLA adapts cheaply when three pretraining choices are made deliberately: the **lineage** (initial weights × further-pretrain corpus), the **data composition** (an egocentric-centric corpus matching the in-house acquisition plan, vs an everything-mixed dump), and the **staged recipe / curriculum** — after which the **post-pretraining adaptation range** (freeze / PEFT) and its prior-preservation strategy merely *protect* what pretraining already bought. Preservation is one downstream lever, not the headline. Task specification stays goal-centric (arm-hand integrated).

**Decomposition (P4-relevant)**
- *Antagonist*: deploy-fine-tuning papers with no pretraining-composition lever; "scale the deploy data" framings that ignore the corpus/lineage; pick-and-place-only VLAs with no adaptation-efficiency or generalization measurement
- *Protagonist (P4 owns)*: a chosen VLM lineage × a deliberately composed (egocentric-centric) pretraining corpus × a staged recipe → cheap adaptation; preservation (freeze/PEFT/conservative-SFT) protects the pretrained prior as a sub-lever
- *Coupling*: the corpus is scouted by **P0** (datasets) and feeds D22; a world-model objective can *be* a pretraining signal (**P5**); the P1 split heads (D4/D7) are the action-side adapter the preservation choice gates

> **Note**: P4 owns the pretraining-recipe / adaptation half. The decoder split
> (P1), observation fusion (P2), System0 (P3), datasets (P0), world model (P5)
> are out of scope here — see `context/MASTER.md`.

## 2. Pillar P4 — Pretraining for Data-Efficient Adaptation [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Scope**: decide the **VLM backbone lineage** (= initial weights × further-pretrain corpus), the **pretraining data composition** (egocentric-only vs mixed human+robot dump — an *open* variable matching the long-term ego acquisition plan), the **staged training recipe / curriculum**, and the **post-pretraining adaptation range** (freeze / partial / LoRA-adapter / full) with its **prior-preservation strategy** (a sub-lever, not the headline). The lineage and the multi-embodiment pretraining-data catalog are first-class P4 variables. Pretraining-data scouting is shared with P0; a world-model pretraining objective is coordinated with P5.

**Identity tie**: pretraining quality/composition is the upstream lever behind data-efficient adaptation — the thing that lets minutes of deploy data suffice and that the whole VLA-level pivot rests on.

**Tracked items**: VLM backbone lineage + post-pretraining adaptation range (D19), prior-preservation strategy (D20), staged pretraining + adaptation recipe (D21), pretraining data composition — egocentric vs mixed (D22), action-representation × pretraining/preservation (D23).

**Anti-topics**: deploy-fine-tuning papers with no pretraining-composition / lineage lever; pick-and-place-only VLA with no adaptation-efficiency or forgetting measurement; full-FT results with no prior-retention / generalization metric.

**Literature anchor**: π0 / π0.5 (π lineage), GR00T N1 (cross-embodiment lineage), Being-H0.5 (human-video-centric pretraining), Xiaomi-Robotics-0 (open-weight lineage), Qwen-VLA (Qwen lineage + text-to-action pretraining), ConSFT (conservative adaptation recipe). See §5.
