## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: the questions **upstream of deploy**, and what they cost to protect
afterwards. The **corpus** — what the further-pretraining (mid-training)
data is made of and what is extracted from it, first-person worker video
above all. The **staged recipe** and its curriculum. How **action experts**
come out of the backbone. The **action representation** pretraining has to
mesh with. And downstream of those, how far the backbone is opened at
adaptation and how the prior is protected — which only protects what
pretraining already bought. Corpus scouting is shared with P0; a world-model
objective that doubles as a pretraining signal is coordinated with P3.

**Thesis tie** — why this axis is asked: how much deploy data an adaptation
needs, and how much the backbone forgets, may be set one stage earlier — by
the corpus and the recipe it was pretrained on — or at adaptation itself, and
which stage holds the lever is open. Scaling the deploy data and protecting
the prior at adaptation stay threads, the baselines a composed pretraining is
measured against.

**Questions**: how far the backbone opens at adaptation and what protects the
prior (D3RP), how the stages are laid out (D3WV), what the mid-training
corpus is and what it yields (D9QJ), how action experts come out of the
backbone (D6JW), which action representation meshes with pretraining (D1WE).

**Premises** — a lineage is written `initial weights × further-pretrain
corpus`, never as the bare model name.

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **A pretraining choice varied and measured** — lineage, corpus
   composition, or staged recipe changed upstream, with adaptation cost
   reported downstream. The north star.
2. **Anything that measures what a pretrained prior holds** — forgetting,
   where capacity sits in the stack, prior retention under fine-tuning,
   adaptation range, action-representation compatibility. The evidence pool:
   a recipe cannot be chosen without knowing what it buys and what survives.
3. **Deploy-side fine-tuning results** — the comparison group, and only when
   a retention or generalization number is reported beside the target-task
   score.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P4
or a P4 question):

- Deploy-fine-tuning papers with no pretraining-composition / lineage /
  data-curriculum lever
- Pick-and-place-only VLA with no adaptation-efficiency or forgetting /
  over-specialization analysis
- Full-FT VLA results with no prior-retention / generalization measurement
- Action-only papers ignoring the backbone and its pretraining entirely
- Continual-learning / PEFT outside the VLA-pretraining context
  (cross-pollination only — `context/MASTER.md` §5)
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Open Questions — P4 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The questions this pillar keeps open. Each names what it turns on and the
threads the corpus has followed so far; none is waiting to be closed, and a
thread is added when a paper opens one.

#### [D3RP] 적응할 때 백본을 어디까지 학습시키고, 사전학습으로 얻은 것은 어떻게 지키나 (P4)
- Opening more of the backbone fits the target task and spends what pretraining bought. Threads: the VLM frozen with only action experts trained; partial, LoRA or full adaptation; an action-side adapter with conservative SFT (ConSFT); capacity located by removing blocks first (Drop-Then-Recovery).
#### [D3WV] 사전학습에서 배포까지 학습을 어떤 단계로 나누나 (P4)
- Each stage decides what the next can rely on, and the order of data is a lever of its own. Threads: mid-training on a composed corpus, experts trained over a stable VLM, conservative deploy adaptation; an ego→robot curriculum; one joint stage on a mixture (FACT).
#### [D9QJ] 중간 학습(mid-training) 데이터는 무엇으로 구성하고, 1인칭 작업자 영상에서는 무엇을 뽑아내나 (P4)
- First-person worker video is plentiful and keeps being released, but carries no action labels and no force. Threads: an egocentric-centric corpus; an everything-mixed corpus (GR00T N1); hand keypoints and contact from a labeler whose quality moves results (RLHND, Dexterous Point Policy); joint affordances (VLAff); hand-surface latent actions (GALA); 3D traces (μ0).
#### [D6JW] 행동 전문가를 사전학습 백본에서 어떻게 얻나 — 이어받기, 곁에 새로 두기, 처음부터 학습 (P4)
- Where the action experts come from decides how much of the pretrained prior they keep. Threads: initialising from π0's action expert, sliced into parts where the decoder is split, and fine-tuning it; leaving π0's action expert frozen and training new experts beside it; training the action side from scratch.
#### [D1WE] 행동을 어떻게 표현해야 사전학습의 신호가 잘 전달되나 — 연속값, 이산 코드, 잠재 표현 (P4)
- The action representation decides which pretraining signals reach the action expert. Threads: a continuous flow-matching head (π0); discrete hand codes (DQ-RISE); discrete latent actions as auxiliary supervision only (GALA); a cross-hand latent (XL-VLA).
