## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: the questions of how a predictive model of environment dynamics
folds into a hand-centric VLA stack. **Role** — what the prediction is for:
a prior and auxiliary co-trained with the policy, a goal supplier, an
environment to evaluate or train in, or rollouts to learn from.
**Integration** — where it attaches. **Prediction space and subject** — what
is predicted, from latents to pixels, and whether egocentric hand-object
interaction is the thing predicted. **Conditioning** — whether the action is
an input. A world-model objective that doubles as a pretraining signal is
coordinated with P2.

**Thesis tie** — why this axis is asked: a VLA learns *what action* to emit
and rarely *what happens next*, and whether a pretrained world model's
forward sense turns into data-efficient adaptation is open — for prediction
a contact-rich policy can act on.

**Questions**: what the world model is for (D3FQ), where it attaches (D8XB),
what it predicts (D9KS), whether it is action-conditioned (D6FM).

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **Prediction a policy acts on, measured on a manipulation task** —
   whatever the prediction space or conditioning, as long as the policy's
   result is reported with and without it. The north star.
2. **Any predictive objective coupled to control** — auxiliary heads, JEPA
   latent prediction, video pretraining that transfers, a world model
   supplying goals to a policy beneath it — action-free pretraining included
   when the transfer is shown.
3. **Generative world models with no policy result yet** — raw-pixel and
   video models, tracked as the realism and cost reference for D9KS's
   threads, not rejected.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P4
or a P4 question):

- Action-free video generation with no robot transfer
- Driving / navigation / autonomous-vehicle world models
- Locomotion / quadruped model-based RL world models
- World models with no manipulation evaluation
- Pure generative-video architecture papers with no embodiment or policy use
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Open Questions — P4 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The questions this pillar keeps open. Each names what it turns on and the
threads the corpus has followed so far; none is waiting to be closed, and a
thread is added when a paper opens one.

#### [D3FQ] 세계 모델은 정책에 무엇을 해 주나 — 사전 지식, 목표, 평가 환경, 학습 데이터 (P4)
- A forward model can shape the policy's representation, hand it goals, or stand in for the world. Threads: a dynamics prior and future-prediction auxiliary co-trained with the policy (VT-WAM); a goal supplier to a residual policy beneath it (TouchWorld); evaluation in imagination (Ctrl-World); an RL environment; rollout synthesis.
#### [D8XB] 세계 모델을 정책의 어디에 붙이나 — 보조 헤드로, 한 모델로 합쳐, 따로 학습한 뒤 이어서 (P4)
- Where the prediction attaches decides what it can change and what it can disturb. Threads: an auxiliary head on the shared backbone (Being-H0.7); one autoregressive model for actions and images (WorldVLA); pretrain the predictor, freeze it, then attach a fresh action expert (μ0, VLA-JEPA).
#### [D9KS] 세계 모델은 무엇을 예측하나 — 잠재 표현, 3D 궤적, 접촉 변형, 픽셀, 손과 물체의 상호작용 (P4)
- The prediction space trades realism against compute and against what a contact-rich policy can use. Threads: latent prediction (VLA-JEPA); sparse 3D interaction traces (μ0); contact deformation (VT-WAM); raw pixels (TouchWorld, Ctrl-World); egocentric hand-object interaction as the subject (DexWM, LOME).
#### [D6FM] 세계 모델의 예측은 행동을 입력으로 받아야 하나 (P4)
- Conditioning on the action makes the model answer what happens if, and ties it to action labels. Threads: per-frame action-conditioned prediction (LOME, DexWM); action-free pretraining whose features transfer anyway (μ0); action predicted jointly with the future (VT-WAM).
