## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: the choices made **upstream of deploy**, and what they cost to
protect afterwards. The **lineage** — initial weights × further-pretrain
corpus, which is what a lineage *is*, not the bare model name. The **data
composition** — an egocentric-centric corpus matching the planned egocentric acquisition
plan against an everything-mixed dump, the pillar's open question. The
**staged recipe** and its curriculum. And downstream of those, the
**adaptation range** (freeze / partial / LoRA / full) with its
**prior-preservation strategy**, which only protects what pretraining already
bought. Corpus scouting is shared with P0; a world-model objective that
doubles as a pretraining signal is coordinated with P3.

**Thesis tie**: minutes of deploy data suffice only when the pretraining was
composed for it — so the lever is one stage earlier than the symptom.
Catastrophic forgetting and "just scale the deploy data" are both downstream
of a corpus and a lineage chosen on purpose, and preservation is a sub-lever,
not the headline.

**Tracked items**: VLM backbone lineage + post-pretraining adaptation range
(D3RP), prior-preservation strategy (D5ZL), staged pretraining + adaptation
recipe (D3WV), pretraining data composition — egocentric vs mixed (D9QJ),
action-representation × pretraining/preservation (D1WE).

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **A pretraining choice varied and measured** — lineage, corpus
   composition, or staged recipe changed upstream, with adaptation cost
   reported downstream. The north star.
2. **Anything that measures what a pretrained prior holds** — forgetting,
   where capacity sits in the stack, prior retention under fine-tuning,
   adaptation range, action-representation compatibility. The evidence pool:
   a recipe cannot be chosen without knowing what it buys and what survives.
3. **Deploy-side fine-tuning results** — the comparison group, and only when
   a retention or generalization number is reported beside the target-task
   score.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P4
or a P4 Decision):

- Deploy-fine-tuning papers with no pretraining-composition / lineage /
  data-curriculum lever
- Pick-and-place-only VLA with no adaptation-efficiency or forgetting /
  over-specialization analysis
- Full-FT VLA results with no prior-retention / generalization measurement
- Action-only papers ignoring the backbone and its pretraining entirely
- Continual-learning / PEFT outside the VLA-pretraining context
  (cross-pollination only — `context/MASTER.md` §6)
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Decision Log — P4 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The current choice per decision, **bold** on the chosen alternative, deferred
alternatives inline; rationale lives outside this file.
P4 owns **D3RP–D1WE** — the full allocation is in `context/CLAUDE.md`.

#### [D3RP] VLM backbone — lineage & adaptation range (P4)
- **lineage** = PaliGemma-2B × π0 mix (= openpi weights); a lineage = `(initial weights) × (further-pretrain corpus)`, not the bare model name (open-weight candidates Qwen-VLA / Xiaomi-Robotics-0 tracked). **Adaptation range** = (a) full VLM freeze + action experts only (protect the pretrained prior); partial / LoRA / full deferred behind an insufficiency trigger
#### [D5ZL] Prior-preservation strategy (sub-lever) (P4)
- action-side adapter (the P1 D9ZP/D6JW split heads *are* the adapter; backbone untouched) + conservative SFT at the adaptation stage. Standby while D3RP=(a) freeze holds
#### [D3WV] Staged pretraining + adaptation recipe (P4)
- Stage 0 lineage selection → Stage 1 (further-)pretrain on the composed corpus (D9QJ) → Stage 2 VLM-stable + train Body/Hand experts → Stage 3 conservative deploy adaptation (ConSFT-style). Curriculum (ego→robot ordering) deferred but tracked
#### [D9QJ] Pretraining data composition — egocentric vs mixed (P4) — **OPEN**
- **Working, not settled** — **egocentric-centric** corpus (matching the egocentric acquisition plan, P0 D4ML) + targeted robot-action + mixed retarget. The **everything-mixed dump vs egocentric-only** question is an explicit *open ablation*, not yet decided — held as a tracked variable.
#### [D1WE] Action representation × pretraining/preservation (P4)
- (iii) continuous flow-matching head (π-consistent; VLM kept in its semantic role; compatible with D3RP freeze)
