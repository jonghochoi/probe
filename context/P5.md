## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: the questions about how human motion becomes robot training data
for a multi-finger hand — what a **retargeting map** keeps when it moves a
human hand onto a robot hand, how many retargeted trajectories survive to be
usable and what that **yield** costs, how a **human-in-the-loop** collection
hands control between a running policy and an operator, and where an
**operator's time** buys the most improvement: a demonstration, a correction
or a label on an autonomous rollout. A paper lands here when the collection
step itself is what it varies or measures; which corpora exist is P0's, and
how they are composed for pretraining is P4's.

**Thesis tie** — why this axis is asked: a policy trained by imitation sees
only the robot data a human's motion was turned into, and how much of what it
learns is set at that step — by what the retargeting map keeps, how many
trajectories survive it, and how a human enters the collection loop — is open.
Fingertip task-vector matching and demonstrations alone stay threads, the
baselines a designed map or loop is measured against. What P5 finds bears on
the data P0 scouts (D4ML), the corpus P4 composes (D9QJ) and the action space
P1 asks the data to carry (D1QT).

**Questions**: what a retargeting map keeps (D2KT), what sets retargeting
yield (D7HF), how a handover is joined (D5VN), where operator time goes
(D8LC).

**Premises** — the hand is the one `context/P2.md` §1 Premises sets out.

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **Collection measured by what a policy learns** — a retargeting map, a
   teleop interface or a human-in-the-loop protocol for a multi-finger hand,
   judged by the policy trained on its data or by usable data per unit of
   cost. The north star.
2. **Retargeting and teleop systems measured on their own terms** — tracking
   error, teleop success, handover continuity, trajectories accepted per
   unit of compute. The evidence pool.
3. **Pipelines that use a retargeter or an intervention loop as a
   component** — surfaced as the comparison group: which map and which loop
   the corpus actually runs on.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to
P5 or a P5 question):

- Body or humanoid motion retargeting with no fingers in the loop
- Teleop hardware design (gloves, exoskeletons) with no data or policy
  measurement
- Parallel-jaw intervention or DAgger studies with nothing that transfers to
  a multi-finger hand
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Open Questions — P5 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The questions this pillar keeps open. Each names what it turns on and the
threads the corpus has followed so far; none is waiting to be closed, and a
thread is added when a paper opens one.

#### [D2KT] 사람 손동작을 로봇 손으로 옮길 때 무엇을 지켜야 하나 — 손끝 위치, 움직임 방향, 접촉 위치 (P5)
- The hands differ in size, proportion and finger count, so every map gives something up; what it keeps is what the data teaches. Threads: scaled fingertip task vectors (DexPilot, AnyTeleop); geometric properties, no paired data (Geometric Retargeting); a morphed human hand with contacts recovered (Morphometric Imitation); contact positions instead of joints (2609.24093); hand-object mesh deformation (Regrind).
#### [D7HF] 옮긴 궤적 중 학습에 쓸 만한 것은 얼마나 되고, 그 비율은 무엇이 정하나 (P5)
- A kinematic retarget can look right and still penetrate, slip or leave the object unmoved, and making it valid costs compute per trajectory. Threads: per-trajectory physics optimization by sampling MPC; a generator learned from solved seeds, sampled and filtered (GNR); a residual policy over the kinematic reference in simulation (Morphometric Imitation, Regrind); contact force recovered in simulation (Dex-X).
#### [D5VN] 정책이 하던 조작을 사람이 넘겨받는 순간을 어떻게 잇나 (P5)
- Handover joins two commands that disagree, and what is recorded right after it can be realignment rather than correction. Threads: absolute hand commands kept, with rollback, pose alignment and a blend while the raw operator command is recorded (2609.19666); the operator matching the policy's pose first, relative arm and smoothed hand substitution (DexPIE); relative hand retargeting with shared arm control.
#### [D8LC] 사람의 시간을 어디에 써야 데이터가 가장 좋아지나 — 시연, 실패 교정, 자율 롤아웃 라벨링 (P5)
- An operator-hour can buy a demonstration, a correction on the policy's own failure or a label on an autonomous rollout, and each improves a different thing. Threads: more demonstrations; human-gated DAgger corrections (2609.19666); corrections from restored intermediate stages with a critic over them (DexPIE); autonomous rollouts for residual RL (2609.19666, LAMP).
