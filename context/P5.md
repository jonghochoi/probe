## 1. Identity [STABLE] [AGENT-INPUT]

> A VLA learns *what action* to emit; it rarely learns *what happens next*. A world model — a predictive model of environment dynamics conditioned on action — supplies that missing forward sense, and the labs that pretrain it heavily (Genesis AI–style) convert it into data-efficient adaptation. P5 asks **how to fold a world model into the hand-centric VLA stack**: as a latent dynamics prior / future-prediction auxiliary co-trained with the policy, as eval-in-imagination, or as an RL environment. The hand-centric bet narrows the target to **action-conditioned, egocentric, hand-object world models** — contact-relevant prediction (latent / 3D-flow) over raw-pixel generation. Task specification stays goal-centric (arm-hand integrated).

**Decomposition (P5-relevant)**
- *Antagonist*: pure video generation with no action conditioning and no robot transfer; driving / navigation world models; model-based RL for locomotion; world models without a manipulation eval
- *Protagonist (P5 owns)*: action-conditioned egocentric hand-object world model — latent / 3D-flow prediction co-trained with the VLA as a dynamics prior + future-prediction auxiliary; eval-in-imagination and RL-env as deferred roles
- *Coupling*: shares the backbone + pretraining corpus with P4 (the world-model objective is a pretraining signal), the egocentric corpus with P0, and the contact/3D-flow representation with P2/P3

> **Note**: P5 owns the predictive-dynamics half. Architecture (P1),
> observation fusion (P2), System0 (P3), pretraining recipe (P4), data (P0) are
> out of scope here — see `context/MASTER.md`.

## 2. Pillar P5 — World Model [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Scope**: world-model **role** (latent dynamics prior / future-prediction auxiliary / eval-in-imagination / RL-env / data-augmentation); **integration architecture** (auxiliary head on the shared VLA backbone vs fully unified autoregressive VLA+WM); **prediction space** (raw-pixel video / latent / 3D-flow); **action conditioning** (per-frame action-conditioned prediction); and **egocentric hand-object** world models bridging human video to the in-house ego plan. World-model objectives that double as a pretraining signal are coordinated with P4.

**Identity tie**: forward dynamics is the predictive sense a VLA lacks; heavily-pretrained world models are the lever behind Genesis-style data-efficient adaptation — scoped here to action-conditioned, egocentric, hand-object prediction.

**Tracked items**: world-model role (D28), integration architecture (D29), prediction space (D30), action conditioning (D31), egocentric hand-object world model (D32).

**Anti-topics**: action-free video generation without robot transfer; driving / navigation world models; locomotion model-based RL; world models with no manipulation eval.

**Literature anchor**: DexWM (hand-object WM from human video), Being-H0.7 (latent world-action from ego video), WorldVLA (unified autoregressive VLA+WM), LOME (action-conditioned egocentric WM), AHEAD (latent-space predictive WM for dynamic VLA), VLA-JEPA (JEPA latent-prediction world model for VLA), Ctrl-World (controllable generative raw-pixel WM / eval-in-imagination), World Guidance (WAM condition-space → action). See §5.

## 3. Decision Log — P5 / World Model (D28–D32) [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

v1 choice per decision (first-attempt default; rationale and deferred candidates
held outside the context file). P5 covers **D28–D32**; P0 (D24–D27), P1 (D1–D7),
P2 (D8–D12), P3/System0 (D13–D18), P4 (D19–D23) are out of scope here — see

> **Phasing note (P5-owned)**: world-model integration is a *later-phase*
> capability bet; the v1 choices set direction, not current implementation.
> The architectural-validation phases (P1–P3 demos) precede a world-model add.

#### [D28] World-model role (P5)
- **v1**: latent **dynamics prior + future-prediction auxiliary** co-trained with the VLA (not a standalone planner). Eval-in-imagination and RL-environment roles deferred; data-augmentation (rollout synthesis) tracked
#### [D29] Integration architecture (P5)
- **v1**: world-model objective as an **auxiliary head on the shared VLA backbone** (Being-H0.7 latent world-action style), preserving the action expert. Fully **unified autoregressive VLA+WM** (WorldVLA) tracked as the alternative — single model, attention-mask interference risk. VLA-JEPA's **two-stage** (JEPA pretrain → action-head fine-tune) is a decoupled variant of the auxiliary route
#### [D30] Prediction space (P5)
- **v1**: **latent / 3D-flow** prediction (contact-relevant, compute-bounded; DynaFLIP-consistent with P2). **VLA-JEPA** is the pinned instantiation of this latent choice (ThinkJEPA / JEPA-VLA tracked as methodology base). Raw-pixel video generation deferred (cost) but tracked for eval-in-imagination realism
#### [D31] Action conditioning (P5)
- **v1**: **action-conditioned** (per-frame action) prediction (LOME / DexWM lineage). Action-free next-frame prediction excluded by §4 anti-topics
#### [D32] World model × egocentric hand-object (P5)
- **v1**: prioritize **hand-object-interaction world models from egocentric human video** (DexWM lineage), bridging to the in-house ego acquisition (P0/P4). Third-person object-centric world models down-weighted
