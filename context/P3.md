## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: how a predictive model of environment dynamics is folded into the
hand-centric VLA stack, on four axes. **Role** — a latent dynamics prior and
future-prediction auxiliary co-trained with the policy, against
eval-in-imagination, an RL environment, or rollout synthesis.
**Integration** — an auxiliary head on the shared backbone, a fully unified
autoregressive VLA+WM, or a two-stage pretrain-then-attach. **Prediction
space** — latent, 3D-flow, contact deformation, or raw pixels. **Conditioning
and subject** — per-frame action conditioning, and egocentric hand-object
interaction as the thing predicted. A world-model objective that doubles as a
pretraining signal is coordinated with P4.

**Thesis tie**: a VLA learns *what action* to emit and rarely *what happens
next*, and that missing forward sense is what heavily-pretrained world models
convert into data-efficient adaptation. The hand-centric bet narrows it to
prediction a contact-rich policy can act on. A later-phase capability bet: the
choices below set direction, not current implementation, and the
architectural-validation demos precede a world-model add.

**Tracked items**: world-model role (D3FQ), integration architecture (D8XB),
prediction space (D9KS), action conditioning (D6FM), egocentric hand-object
world model (D7VC).

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **Prediction a policy acts on** — action-conditioned, contact-relevant
   (latent, 3D-flow, tactile), measured on a manipulation task, egocentric
   hand-object first. The north star.
2. **Any predictive objective coupled to control** — auxiliary heads, JEPA
   latent prediction, video pretraining that transfers, a world model
   supplying goals to a policy beneath it — whatever the prediction space,
   and action-free pretraining included when the transfer is shown.
3. **Raw-pixel generative world models** — tracked as the realism and cost
   reference the latent choice is judged against, not rejected.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P3
or a P3 Decision):

- Action-free video generation with no robot transfer
- Driving / navigation / autonomous-vehicle world models
- Locomotion / quadruped model-based RL world models
- World models with no manipulation evaluation
- Pure generative-video architecture papers with no embodiment or policy use
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Decision Log — P3 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The current choice per decision, **bold** on the chosen alternative, deferred
alternatives inline; rationale lives outside this file.
P3 owns **D3FQ–D7VC** — the full allocation is in `context/CLAUDE.md`.

#### [D3FQ] World-model role (P3)
- latent **dynamics prior + future-prediction auxiliary** co-trained with the VLA (not a standalone planner). Eval-in-imagination and RL-environment roles deferred; data-augmentation (rollout synthesis) tracked
#### [D8XB] Integration architecture (P3)
- world-model objective as an **auxiliary head on the shared VLA backbone** (Being-H0.7 latent world-action style), preserving the action expert. Fully **unified autoregressive VLA+WM** (WorldVLA) tracked as the alternative — single model, attention-mask interference risk. VLA-JEPA's **two-stage** (JEPA pretrain → action-head fine-tune) is a decoupled variant of the auxiliary route
#### [D9KS] Prediction space (P3)
- **latent / 3D-flow** prediction (contact-relevant, compute-bounded; DynaFLIP-consistent with P2). **VLA-JEPA** is the pinned instantiation of this latent choice. Raw-pixel video generation deferred (cost) but tracked for eval-in-imagination realism
#### [D6FM] Action conditioning (P3)
- **action-conditioned** (per-frame action) prediction (LOME / DexWM lineage). Action-free next-frame prediction excluded by the §1 anti-topics
#### [D7VC] World model × egocentric hand-object (P3)
- prioritize **hand-object-interaction world models from egocentric human video** (DexWM lineage), bridging to the in-house ego acquisition (P0/P4). Third-person object-centric world models down-weighted
