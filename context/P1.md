## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: how the action decoder is **decomposed into more than one expert**,
and along which seam. The seam is **anatomical**: a Body expert for macro
motion (object approach, transport, placement) against a Hand expert for
post-contact, contact-rich precision — Body output as **both-wrist /
tool-flange pose** (embodiment-transfer easing), Hand output as **finger joint
command**. Everything that follows is P1's: information sharing across the
seam, input-modality and control-rate separation, coordination direction and
flow, and how the split is carved out of the π backbone. Whether a **second,
temporal seam** belongs beneath it — a contact loop running faster than the
policy — is P1's open question (D5DQ), not a commitment.

**Thesis tie**: a monolithic decoder treats arm, torso and finger as one
homogeneous action space, and that homogeneity is what caps dexterity. So the
decomposition *is* the differentiation claim, not an implementation detail.

**Tracked items** — the anatomical seam: split form (D3GS), Body output space
(D4QX), Hand output space (D1QT), Body↔Hand information sharing (D9ZP),
input-modality + control-rate separation (D1MZ), coordination direction & flow
(D5SH), π backbone integration / partition (D6JW). Open: whether a
sub-policy-rate contact loop earns a second seam at all (D5DQ).

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **Arm/hand dedicated experts** — a decoder split along the anatomical
   seam. The north star; a paper here is P1's regardless of its headline
   framing. A paper that *measures* what a policy-rate loop can and cannot
   hold reads on D5DQ and lands here too.
2. **Any other multi-expert decomposition** — two or more experts on some
   other seam: a tactile expert beside a vision-driven one, a fast loop beside
   a slow backbone, per-skill experts, router/MoE action heads, hierarchical
   action experts. The evidence pool —
   what a seam buys and what it costs transfers across seams.
3. **Monolithic single-expert decoders** and action-space (joint vs task vs
   flange) studies — surfaced as the **comparison group**, scored against the
   split, not rejected.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P1
or a P1 Decision):

- Post-hoc correction / residual on a frozen VLA that does not address the
  distribution-bound limitation
- Flat-concat multimodal fusion presented as a decoder contribution
  (structured binding is P2's)
- Pick-and-place-only VLAs with no action-architecture / action-space
  contribution
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)
