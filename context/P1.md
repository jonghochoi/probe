## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: the questions of **how an action decoder is decomposed into more
than one expert**, and along which seam — by body part, by embodiment, by
skill, by modality, or in time — and the questions that stand whether it is
split or not: what the policy sends to each actuator group, how the experts
are coupled, and on what clock signals arrive and actions leave. How a split
is carved out of a pretrained backbone is P4's (D6JW).

**Thesis tie** — why this axis is asked: a single decoder treats every
actuated part — base, legs, torso, arms, fingers — as one action space, and
whether that homogeneity limits dexterity, and which split would lift it, is
open. An undivided decoder stays a thread of D3GS, the baseline any split is
measured against; a paper that measures one against the other is evidence
either way.

**Questions**: whether and where to split the decoder (D3GS), what leaves
the policy for the actuators (D1QT), how the parts are coupled (D9ZP), on
what clock signals arrive and actions leave (D1MZ).

**Premises** — the hand is the one `context/P2.md` §1 Premises sets out.

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **Body-part experts** — a decoder whose experts own different parts of
   the action output: arm and hand, one arm and the other, locomotion and
   manipulation, or any other partition of the actuated body. The north star;
   a paper here is P1's regardless of its headline framing. A paper that
   *measures* what a policy-rate loop can and cannot hold bears on D1MZ and
   lands here too.
2. **Multi-expert decomposition on any other seam** — two or more experts
   that share one action output but split it some other way: a tactile
   expert beside a vision-driven one, a fast loop beside a slow backbone,
   per-skill experts, router/MoE action heads, hierarchical action experts.
   The evidence pool — what a seam buys and what it costs transfers across
   seams.
3. **Monolithic single-expert decoders** and action-space (joint vs task vs
   flange) studies — surfaced as the **comparison group**: any split is
   measured against them, so they are read, not rejected.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P1
or a P1 question):

- Post-hoc correction / residual on a frozen VLA with no action-decoder or
  action-space contribution
- Flat-concat multimodal fusion presented as a decoder contribution
  (structured binding is P2's)
- Pick-and-place-only VLAs with no action-architecture / action-space
  contribution
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Open Questions — P1 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The questions this pillar keeps open. Each names what it turns on and the
threads the corpus has followed so far; none is waiting to be closed, and a
thread is added when a paper opens one.

#### [D3GS] 행동 디코더를 나눠야 하나, 나눈다면 무엇을 기준으로 (P1)
- One network treats every actuated part as one action space; a split buys specialization and costs coordination. Threads: per-body-part heads over a shared trunk; per-embodiment heads (GALA); per-skill experts distilled into one policy (UniCross); router / MoE action heads; no split.
#### [D1QT] 정책은 실행기로 무엇을 내보내나 — 부위별 행동 공간 (P1)
- What leaves the policy decides what transfers across bodies and how much detail reaches each actuator group, split or not. Threads: arm — both-wrist / tool-flange pose, arm joint command; hand — finger joint command, a cross-hand latent replacing joints (XL-VLA), fingertip keypoints mapped by IK (Dexterous Point Policy), discrete per-task hand codes (DQ-RISE).
#### [D9ZP] 전문가들은 어떻게 엮이나 — 무엇이, 어느 방향으로 건너가나 (P1)
- The parts must agree on one motion whether they sit in separate heads or one network; what passes between them, and which way, fixes what each can count on. Threads: one part's action modulating another's input at one FiLM point, which carries what but not when (InDex); top-down hierarchy (Body → Hand); bottom-up feedback; cross-attention; a shared trunk alone.
#### [D1MZ] 신호는 어떤 시계로 들어오고, 행동은 어떤 시계로 나가나 (P1)
- Modalities arrive at different rates, contact changes faster than vision, and execution speed is set somewhere. Threads: one shared rate; proprioception every tick, vision-language asynchronous (πR²); tactile refreshed inside the chunk (TacForcing); a tactile expert re-denoising mid-chunk (T-Rex); a reflex loop below the policy; speed baked into the data or taken as an input.
