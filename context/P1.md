## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: how the action decoder is **decomposed into more than one expert**,
and along which seams. Two are in scope. The **anatomical** seam splits a Body
expert for macro motion (object approach, transport, placement) from a Hand
expert for post-contact, contact-rich precision — Body output as **both-wrist
/ tool-flange pose** (embodiment-transfer easing), Hand output as **finger
joint command**. The **temporal** seam splits that policy-rate decoder from a
faster **contact-reaction sub-loop** that takes finger commands over while a
grasp must be held and hands them back when it must not. Everything either
seam implies is P1's: information sharing across the seam, input-modality and
control-rate separation, coordination direction and flow, the gate that opens
and closes the fast loop and what that loop is allowed to see, and how the
split is carved out of the π backbone.

**Thesis tie**: a monolithic decoder treats arm, torso and finger as one
homogeneous action space running at one rate, and that homogeneity — of
anatomy and of timing alike — is what caps dexterity. So the decomposition
*is* the differentiation claim, not an implementation detail.

**Tracked items** — the anatomical seam: split form (D3GS), Body output space
(D4QX), Hand output space (D1QT), Body↔Hand information sharing (D9ZP),
input-modality + control-rate separation (D1MZ), coordination direction & flow
(D5SH), π backbone integration / partition (D6JW). The temporal seam: the
contact-reaction sub-loop's role and operating regime (D5DQ), the gate between
it and the policy-rate decoder (D6ZD), what the sub-loop sees (D4NT) and what it
emits (D5QT).

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **A seam P1 owns** — arm/hand dedicated experts first, a policy-rate /
   contact-rate split second. The north star; a paper here is P1's regardless
   of its headline framing.
2. **Any other multi-expert decomposition** — two or more experts on some
   other seam: a tactile expert beside a vision-driven one, per-skill experts,
   router/MoE action heads, hierarchical action experts. The evidence pool —
   what a seam buys and what it costs transfers across seams.
3. **Monolithic single-expert decoders** and action-space (joint vs task vs
   flange) studies — surfaced as the **comparison group**, scored against the
   split, not rejected.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P1
or a P1 Decision):

- Post-hoc correction / residual on a frozen VLA that does not address the
  distribution-bound limitation
- Flat-concat multimodal fusion presented as a decoder contribution
  (structured binding is P2's)
- Pick-and-place-only VLAs with no action-architecture / action-space
  contribution
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)
