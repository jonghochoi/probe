## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: the questions of **how an action decoder is decomposed into more
than one expert**, and along which seam — anatomical (a Body expert for
macro motion, a Hand expert for post-contact precision), by embodiment, by
skill, or in time — and the questions that stand whether it is split or not:
what the policy sends to the actuators, how arm and hand are coupled, and on
what clock signals arrive and actions leave. How a split is carved out of a
pretrained backbone is P4's (D6JW).

**Thesis tie** — the bet this axis tests: a monolithic decoder treats arm,
torso and finger as one homogeneous action space, and that homogeneity is
what caps dexterity, so the decomposition *is* the differentiation claim.
An undivided decoder stays a thread of D3GS, as the baseline the bet is
measured against; a paper that measures it against a split is evidence
either way.

**Questions**: whether and where to split the decoder (D3GS), what leaves
the policy for the actuators (D1QT), how the parts are coupled (D9ZP), on
what clock signals arrive and actions leave (D1MZ).

**Premises** — the setting the questions are asked in, not answers to them:
the hand is the Sharpa Hand (22 DOF, no wrist DOF, fingertip tactile at
~30 Hz; `context/P2.md` §1), and the backbone lineage is π0 (`context/P4.md`
§1 Premises).

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored:

1. **Arm/hand dedicated experts** — a decoder split along the anatomical
   seam. The north star; a paper here is P1's regardless of its headline
   framing. A paper that *measures* what a policy-rate loop can and cannot
   hold bears on D1MZ and lands here too.
2. **Any other multi-expert decomposition** — two or more experts on some
   other seam: a tactile expert beside a vision-driven one, a fast loop beside
   a slow backbone, per-skill experts, router/MoE action heads, hierarchical
   action experts. The evidence pool —
   what a seam buys and what it costs transfers across seams.
3. **Monolithic single-expert decoders** and action-space (joint vs task vs
   flange) studies — surfaced as the **comparison group**: the case for any
   split is argued against them, so they are read, not rejected.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P1
or a P1 question):

- Post-hoc correction / residual on a frozen VLA that does not address the
  distribution-bound limitation
- Flat-concat multimodal fusion presented as a decoder contribution
  (structured binding is P2's)
- Pick-and-place-only VLAs with no action-architecture / action-space
  contribution
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Open Questions — P1 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The questions this pillar keeps open. Each names what it turns on and the
threads the corpus has followed so far; none is waiting to be closed, and a
thread is added when a paper opens one.

#### [D3GS] 행동 디코더를 나눠야 하나, 나눈다면 무엇을 기준으로 (P1)
- One network treats arm, torso and fingers as one action space; a split buys specialization and costs coordination. Threads: anatomical Body/Hand heads over a shared trunk; per-embodiment heads (GALA); per-skill experts distilled into one policy (UniCross); router / MoE action heads; no split.
#### [D1QT] 정책은 실행기로 무엇을 내보내나 — 팔과 손의 행동 공간 (P1)
- What leaves the policy decides what transfers across arms and hands and how much finger detail reaches the actuators, split or not. Threads: arm — both-wrist / tool-flange pose, arm joint command; hand — finger joint command, a cross-hand latent replacing joints (XL-VLA), fingertip keypoints mapped by IK (Dexterous Point Policy), discrete per-task hand codes (DQ-RISE).
#### [D9ZP] 팔과 손은 어떻게 엮이나 — 무엇이, 어느 방향으로 건너가나 (P1)
- Arm and hand must agree on one motion whether they sit in two heads or one network; what passes between them, and which way, fixes what each can count on. Threads: the Body action modulating the Hand input at one FiLM point, which carries what but not when (InDex); Body → Hand hierarchy; Hand → Body feedback; cross-attention; a shared trunk alone.
#### [D1MZ] 신호는 어떤 시계로 들어오고, 행동은 어떤 시계로 나가나 (P1)
- Modalities arrive at different rates, contact changes faster than vision, and execution speed is set somewhere. Threads: one shared rate; proprioception every tick, vision-language asynchronous (πR²); tactile refreshed inside the chunk (TacForcing); a tactile expert re-denoising mid-chunk (T-Rex); a reflex loop below the policy; speed baked into the data or taken as an input.
