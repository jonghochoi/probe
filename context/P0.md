## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: scouting and triage of the corpora and the measuring instruments —
(a) **robot-action** datasets (teleop / sim-augmented, single- to
multi-embodiment), (b) **human-egocentric** video with hand/finger tracking,
(c) **mixed** human→robot retargeted corpora, (d) **tactile / force / torque**
datasets, the scarce contact modality, and (e) **benchmarks and eval
harnesses** for dexterous, contact-rich manipulation in sim and real. Every
entry is tagged with modality coverage, scale, embodiment/DOF, license and
lineage — what stacks on what.

**Thesis tie**: data is upstream of method, so the pretraining corpus (P4 D9QJ)
and the egocentric priority need an evidence-driven front-end rather than
incidental scouting. What P0 finds sets the priority data axis (D4ML), and its
tactile/torque coverage feeds observation fusion (P2) and contact (P3).

**Tracked items**: priority data axis (D4ML), tactile/torque data scouting
(D1AL), benchmark/eval scouting scope (D6SE), license/usability bar (D8SD).

**Scouting lens**: a paper qualifies on what it *releases*, not on what it
claims — a method paper whose real contribution is a public corpus or a
reusable harness is P0's, and a dataset paper with a weak method is still P0's.
Scale alone does not qualify a corpus; coverage of the contact modality, of
egocentric hands, or of an evaluation gap does.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P0
or a P0 Decision):

- Method / architecture papers that release no dataset or benchmark
- Single-task demonstration sets below pretraining scale (unless a benchmark)
- Vision-only corpora pitched as sufficient for contact-rich dexterity
- Non-manipulation corpora (autonomous driving, navigation) unless
  egocentric-hand-relevant
- Pure data-engineering tooling without a released corpus
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Decision Log — P0 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The current choice per decision, **bold** on the chosen alternative, deferred
alternatives inline; rationale lives outside this file.
P0 owns **D4ML–D8SD** — the full allocation is in `context/CLAUDE.md`.

#### [D4ML] Priority data axis (P0)
- **egocentric human video–centric** (the priority data axis), with robot-action and mixed human→robot corpora as supplements. Pure third-person robot-only corpora are tracked but down-weighted vs egocentric + hand-tracking sources
#### [D1AL] Tactile / force / torque data scouting (P0)
- actively scout tactile / 6-axis F/T / joint-torque–bearing datasets (RH20T-class); treat their scarcity as a **first-class gap** (most large corpora are vision-only). Flag any new contact-modality release with high priority
#### [D6SE] Benchmark / eval scouting scope (P0)
- dexterous + contact-rich manipulation benchmarks and VLA **eval harnesses**; track both sim (ManiSkill / Isaac Lab / Robocasa) and real (RoboArena-class) suites. In-hand-rotation / articulated-tool evals (CATFA precedent) prioritized for identity-relevant phasing
#### [D8SD] License / usability bar (P0)
- prefer permissive (Apache-2.0 / CC-BY / MIT); gated corpora (Ego4D / Ego-Exo4D) tracked but flagged ⚠️; record per-dataset license + lineage with every tracked entry. NC-only licenses tracked but flagged for downstream-use risk
