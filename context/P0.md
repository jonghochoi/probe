## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: scouting and triage of the corpora and the measuring instruments —
(a) **robot-action** datasets (teleop / sim-augmented, single- to
multi-embodiment), (b) **human-egocentric** video with hand/finger tracking,
(c) **mixed** human→robot retargeted corpora, (d) **tactile / force / torque**
datasets, the scarce contact modality, and (e) **benchmarks and eval
harnesses** for dexterous, contact-rich manipulation in sim and real. Every
entry is tagged with modality coverage, scale, embodiment/DOF, license and
lineage — what stacks on what.

**Thesis tie**: data is upstream of method, so the pretraining corpus (P4 D9QJ)
and the egocentric priority need an evidence-driven front-end rather than
incidental scouting. What P0 finds sets the priority data axis (D4ML), and its
tactile/torque coverage feeds observation fusion (P2) and contact (P3).

**Tracked items**: priority data axis (D4ML), tactile/torque data scouting
(D1AL), benchmark/eval scouting scope (D6SE), license/usability bar (D8SD).

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored. A paper qualifies on what it *releases*, not on what it claims:

1. **A release that fills a gap** — the contact modality, egocentric hands
   with finger tracking, or an evaluation gap in dexterous, contact-rich
   manipulation. The north star.
2. **Any other released dexterous-manipulation corpus or harness** — robot,
   egocentric or mixed, single- to multi-embodiment. Scale alone does not lift
   one to tier 1.
3. **A method paper whose real contribution is a public corpus or a reusable
   harness** — counted for what it releases; a dataset paper with a weak
   method is still P0's.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P0
or a P0 Decision):

- Method / architecture papers that release no dataset or benchmark
- Single-task demonstration sets below pretraining scale (unless a benchmark)
- Vision-only corpora pitched as sufficient for contact-rich dexterity
- Non-manipulation corpora (autonomous driving, navigation) unless
  egocentric-hand-relevant
- Pure data-engineering tooling without a released corpus
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Decision Log — P0 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The current choice per decision. The entry format and the allocation across
pillars are `context/CLAUDE.md`'s.

#### [D4ML] Priority data axis (P0)
- **Egocentric human video with hand tracking first** — the priority data axis, with robot-action and mixed human→robot corpora as supplements. Tracked: third-person robot-only corpora, down-weighted against egocentric ones.
#### [D1AL] Tactile / force / torque data scouting (P0)
- **Contact-modality corpora as a first-class gap** — tactile, 6-axis F/T and joint-torque datasets (RH20T-class) are scouted actively, and a new contact-modality release ranks high, since most large corpora are vision-only.
#### [D6SE] Benchmark / eval scouting scope (P0)
- **Dexterous, contact-rich benchmarks and VLA eval harnesses, in sim and real** — ManiSkill, Isaac Lab and Robocasa in sim, RoboArena-class suites in real; in-hand rotation and articulated-tool evals (the CATFA precedent) first.
#### [D8SD] License / usability bar (P0)
- **Permissive licenses preferred** (Apache-2.0, CC-BY, MIT) — every tracked corpus records its license and lineage. Tracked: gated corpora (Ego4D, Ego-Exo4D) and NC-only licenses, each flagged for downstream-use risk.
