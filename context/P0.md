## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: the questions about the data and the instruments upstream of
method — which corpora a dexterous VLA can stand on: (a) **robot-action**
datasets (teleop / sim-augmented, single- to multi-embodiment), (b)
**human-egocentric** video with hand/finger tracking, (c) **mixed**
human→robot retargeted corpora, (d) **tactile / force / torque** datasets, the
scarce contact modality; and (e) what **benchmarks and eval harnesses**
measure dexterous, contact-rich manipulation in sim and real. Every entry is
tagged with modality coverage, scale, embodiment/DOF, license and lineage —
what stacks on what.

**Thesis tie** — why this axis is asked: which data a policy is trained on
bounds what any method on top of it can show, so the corpora and the
instruments are scouted on their own rather than met incidentally. What P0
finds bears on which data a policy can stand on (D4ML) and on the pretraining
corpus (P4 D9QJ), and its tactile/torque coverage feeds observation design
(P2) and contact (P3).

**Questions**: which data a dexterous VLA stands on (D4ML), what fills the
contact-modality gap (D1AL), what measures dexterous manipulation (D6SE).

**Premises** — a corpus is usable when its license allows downstream use:
permissive licenses (Apache-2.0, CC-BY, MIT) are preferred, gated corpora
(Ego4D, Ego-Exo4D) and NC-only licenses are tracked but flagged, and every
tracked corpus records its license and lineage.

**Scouting lens** — three tiers, and a paper is placed on this ladder before
it is scored. A paper qualifies on what it *releases*, not on what it claims:

1. **A release that fills a gap** — the contact modality, egocentric hands
   with finger tracking, or an evaluation gap in dexterous, contact-rich
   manipulation. The north star.
2. **Any other released dexterous-manipulation corpus or harness** — robot,
   egocentric or mixed, single- to multi-embodiment. Scale alone does not lift
   one to tier 1.
3. **A method paper whose real contribution is a public corpus or a reusable
   harness** — counted for what it releases; a dataset paper with a weak
   method is still P0's.

Recall runs wide across all three; the ranking anchor stays tier 1.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to P0
or a P0 question):

- Method / architecture papers that release no dataset or benchmark
- Single-task demonstration sets below pretraining scale (unless a benchmark)
- Vision-only corpora pitched as sufficient for contact-rich dexterity
- Non-manipulation corpora (autonomous driving, navigation) unless
  egocentric-hand-relevant
- Pure data-engineering tooling without a released corpus
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)

## 2. Open Questions — P0 [LIVING] [AGENT-INPUT]

The questions this pillar keeps open. Each names what it turns on and the
threads the corpus has followed so far; none is waiting to be closed, and a
thread is added when a paper opens one.

#### [D4ML] 손재주 VLA 는 어떤 데이터 위에 서는가 — 1인칭 사람 영상, 로봇 행동, 섞은 데이터 (P0)
- Robot data is costly per demonstration and human video is plentiful but carries no action labels and no force. Threads: egocentric human video with hand tracking (EgoDex); robot-action corpora; mixed human→robot retargeted corpora (Being-H0.5 / UniHand-2.0); heterogeneous hand logs made usable without retargeting (UniDexTok).
#### [D1AL] 접촉 감각 데이터의 빈자리를 무엇으로 메우나 — 촉각, 힘, 토크 (P0)
- Most large corpora are vision-only, and contact is what dexterity turns on. Threads: corpora with measured tactile or F/T (RH20T, HT-Bench); glove-collected fingertip force at scale (FACT); contact and force inferred from video (RLHND); force recovered in simulation (Dex-X).
#### [D6SE] 손재주와 접촉 조작을 무엇으로 재나 — 벤치마크와 평가 도구 (P0)
- A benchmark decides what counts as progress, and few measure contact-rich, in-hand skill. Threads: modular sim benchmarks decoupling task from embodiment (DexVerse); high-throughput sim suites (ManiSkill 3); unified VLA eval harnesses (vla-eval); tactile encoder benchmarks (HT-Bench); real-robot suites.
